Accessibility shaped by lived experience

We help digital teams build accessible products through expert technical evaluation and authentic testing with disabled and neurodivergent users.

Our work combines deep accessibility expertise with real-world user testing to understand how digital services work in practice across screen readers, Voice Control, magnification, keyboard-only, and switch access.

Our Services

Bespoke accessibility solutions tailored to your product context, audience, and release cycles:

Inclusive Research & User Testing

Task-based studies with disabled and neurodivergent participants using real assistive technologies to uncover genuine lived-experience barriers.

Assistive Technology Health Checks

Rapid expert reviews across screen readers (VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS), Voice Control, magnification, keyboard, and switch navigation to catch blockers early.

Accessibility Audits & Strategy

EAA and WCAG 2.2 AA-aligned reviews that translate complex findings into clear, ticket-ready remediation roadmaps for product teams.

Strategic Procurement Testing

Evaluating third-party software, vendors, and digital tools during procurement to guarantee accessibility compliance before purchase.

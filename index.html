Accessibility shaped by lived experience

We help digital teams build accessible products through expert technical evaluation and authentic testing with real disabled and neurodivergent users.

Our work combines deep accessibility expertise with real-world user testing to understand how digital services work in practice across screen readers, Voice Control, magnification, keyboard-only, and switch access.

Our Capabilities

Bespoke accessibility solutions combining technical WCAG 2.2 AA evaluation with authentic lived experience:

Lived Experience

Inclusive Research & User Testing

Task-based studies with disabled and neurodivergent participants using real assistive technologies to uncover genuine usability barriers across key digital journeys.

Technical Validation

Assistive Technology Health Checks

Rapid expert reviews across screen readers (VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS), Voice Control, magnification, keyboard, and switch navigation to catch blockers early in sprint cycles.

Compliance & EAA

Accessibility Audits & Action Roadmaps

WCAG 2.2 Level AA and EAA-aligned technical reviews that translate complex findings into clear, ticket-ready remediation roadmaps for product and engineering backlogs.

Vendor Assurance

Strategic Procurement & Vendor Testing

Evaluating third-party software, white-label booking tools, and digital vendors before purchase to guarantee accessibility compliance before financial commitment.

Conversion & Opportunity

Granular Journey Discovery & Moderated Research

Driven by product discovery and careful planning, our experienced moderators evaluate broader user journeys at a granular level to uncover friction, workarounds, and points of exclusion.

Innovation & Integrity

AI-Agent Readiness & Human-Verified Assurance

Preparing digital services for emerging AI-agent interactions while protecting products from synthetic persona failures through 100% human-verified testing.

Important to you, important to us

Source Accessibility regularly works with sensitive information, including disability-related details that are treated as special category data under UK GDPR.

  • Data protection & GDPR: We minimise data, store it securely, and only use it for agreed research and testing purposes.
  • Security: Ways of working are aligned with Cyber Essentials good practice to protect client and participant data.
  • Professional standards: Led by an IAAP member with appropriate professional indemnity (£5m) and public liability (£2m) cover for consultancy and research work.
  • Sustainability: Remote-first by default, reducing travel while still enabling meaningful participation from diverse disabled users.

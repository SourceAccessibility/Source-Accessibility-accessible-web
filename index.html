Lived Experience

Inclusive Research & User Testing

In-house studies with disabled and neurodivergent participants, using real assistive technologies to uncover genuine usability barriers across key digital journeys.

Technical Validation

Assistive Technology Health Checks

Expert testing with screen readers including VoiceOver, NVDA and JAWS, alongside Voice Control, magnification, keyboard and switch access. Designed to identify accessibility blockers early and validate how experiences work in practice.

See how experiences work in practice. Read more

Hands-on testing across assistive technologies explores how people access, navigate and interact with digital experiences — identifying compatibility issues, friction and barriers that automated checks or single-technology testing can miss.

Testing is performed in-house, with the option to validate findings with disabled users where real-world user verification is needed. Scope can be shaped around your priorities and timelines, from rapid manual checks to more in-depth assessment. Findings are presented in a clear, focused format, with the technical detail needed for remediation alongside concise insight to support product decisions and wider accessibility understanding.

Lived Experience in Action

See digital accessibility through the eyes of a blind screen reader user. Real assistive technology testing reveals usability barriers, navigation issues and accessibility gaps that automated testing can miss.

Screen Reader Journey with Adi Latif X Source Accessibility

Adi Latif, a blind screen reader user and experienced accessibility tester, demonstrates how iPhone VoiceOver shapes the experience of planning a journey to the Tower of London.

The walkthrough reveals how unlabelled buttons, vague signposting and unclear interactions can turn a simple journey into a frustrating experience — and how accessible design can give screen reader users the clarity and confidence to navigate independently.

What the demonstration reveals

  • Real-world accessibility barriers: How unlabelled controls, unclear states and navigation issues affect screen reader users.
  • Assistive technology in practice: How VoiceOver interacts with the interface and influences the user journey.
  • The value of disabled user testing: Why testing with people who use assistive technology can uncover usability issues automated accessibility testing may miss.

Accessibility isn’t just about passing automated checks. It’s about creating digital experiences that work for real people.

Important to you, important to us

Source Accessibility regularly works with sensitive information, including disability-related details that are treated as special category data under UK GDPR.

  • Data protection & GDPR: We minimise data, store it securely, and only use it for agreed research and testing purposes.
  • Security: Ways of working are aligned with Cyber Essentials good practice to protect client and participant data.
  • Professional standards: Led by an IAAP member with appropriate professional indemnity (£5m) and public liability (£2m) cover for consultancy and research work.
  • Sustainability: Remote-first by default, reducing travel while still enabling meaningful participation from diverse disabled users.

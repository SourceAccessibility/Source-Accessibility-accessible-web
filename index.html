Digital Accessibility shaped by lived experience

We help digital teams build accessible products through expert technical evaluation and testing with our panel of disabled and neurodivergent users.

Expert evaluation. Human evidence. Actionable proof.

We combine accessibility expertise, real assistive technology testing and lived-experience research to understand how digital services work in practice by uncovering barriers, validating what works and turning evidence into practical action.

Talk to us about your project

What we do

Not one-size-fits-all accessibility. Bespoke accessibility expertise, testing and research shaped around your products, your people and what you need to achieve.

From specialist assistive technology testing and disabled-user research to audits, product development and ongoing embedded support, we work alongside digital teams to understand what is happening in practice, turn evidence into action and help improvements stick.

Test & Understand

Find out how digital experiences work in practice.

Inclusive Research & User Testing

In-house studies with disabled and neurodivergent participants, using real assistive technologies to uncover genuine usability barriers across key digital journeys.

Assistive Technology Health Checks

Expert testing with screen readers including VoiceOver, NVDA and JAWS, alongside Voice Control, magnification, keyboard and switch access, to identify blockers and validate how experiences work in practice.

Granular Journey Discovery & Moderated Research

Carefully planned research into digital journeys to uncover friction, workarounds and points of exclusion — revealing opportunities to improve the experience for everyone.

Improve & Embed

Turn evidence into better products, stronger teams and lasting change.

Accessibility Audits & Action Roadmaps

WCAG 2.2 Level AA and EAA-aligned technical reviews that turn complex findings into clear, ticket-ready remediation roadmaps for product and engineering teams.

Inclusive Design & User-Centred Product Development

Expert evaluation and disabled-user research to shape digital products from early concepts through prototypes and live services, testing ideas and validating what works in practice.

Accessibility Partnership & Embedded Support

Flexible, ongoing accessibility expertise alongside your teams, combining advice, reviews, testing, research and practical support as your needs evolve.

Specialist Support

Focused expertise for specific accessibility challenges, teams and emerging needs.

Strategic Procurement & Vendor Testing

Evaluate third-party software and digital vendors with real assistive technologies and disabled testers, so you can understand accessibility and usability before you commit.

Accessibility Strategy, Training & Mentoring

Practical guidance, mentoring, awareness sessions and hands-on learning help teams build accessibility into everyday roles, workflows and decisions.

AI-Agent Readiness & Human-Verified Assurance

Human-verified testing uncovers accessibility and interaction barriers that can affect people and emerging AI agents, helping build more robust digital journeys.

Accessibility Demonstrations, Video & Knowledge Sharing

Live demonstrations, recorded walkthroughs and practical sessions that help teams see accessibility barriers, understand their impact and validate improvements.

Digital Accessibility shaped by lived experience

We help digital teams build accessible products through expert technical evaluation and testing with our panel of disabled and neurodivergent users.

Expert evaluation. Human evidence. Actionable proof.

We combine accessibility expertise, real assistive technology testing and lived-experience research to understand how digital services work in practice by uncovering barriers, validating what works and turning evidence into practical action.

What we do

Not one-size-fits-all accessibility. Bespoke accessibility solutions where technical expertise meets lived experience.

We understand it. We evidence it. We make it actionable.

Lived Experience

Inclusive Research & User Testing

In-house studies with disabled and neurodivergent participants, using real assistive technologies to uncover genuine usability barriers across key digital journeys.

Technical Validation

Assistive Technology Health Checks

Expert testing with screen readers including VoiceOver, NVDA and JAWS, alongside Voice Control, magnification, keyboard and switch access. Designed to identify accessibility blockers early and validate how experiences work in practice.

Compliance & EAA

Accessibility Audits & Action Roadmaps

WCAG 2.2 Level AA and EAA-aligned technical reviews that translate complex findings into clear, ticket-ready remediation roadmaps for product and engineering backlogs.

Vendor Assurance

Strategic Procurement & Vendor Testing

Testing of third-party software, white-label solutions and digital vendors using real assistive technologies and disabled testers — giving you an honest view of accessibility, usability and the wider user experience before you commit.

Conversion & Opportunity

Granular Journey Discovery & Moderated Research

Exploring digital journeys in depth through carefully planned, moderated research to uncover friction, workarounds and points of exclusion — revealing opportunities to improve the experience for everyone.

Innovation & Integrity

AI-Agent Readiness & Human-Verified Assurance

Preparing digital services for emerging AI-agent interactions while protecting products from synthetic persona failures through 100% human-verified testing.

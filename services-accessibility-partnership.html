Accessibility That Fits the Way You Work

Our embedded accessibility consultancy works with your existing product, UX and engineering processes. By agreement, this can include Slack and other communication channels, sprint planning, accessibility acceptance criteria, technical recommendations and Jira-ready findings.

We can also provide accessibility QA support, discuss remediation with teams and validate fixes. Collaboration is available remotely, with in-person support for UK teams where agreed.

Better Context. Better Accessibility Decisions.

Working together over time builds familiarity with your products, journeys, design systems, technical constraints and previous findings. This continuity can reduce repeated discovery, reveal recurring issues and inform recommendations as your products evolve.

Build Capability. Learn from Lived Experience.

Support can include accessibility workshops and training, assistive technology demonstrations, technical mentoring and repeatable accessibility QA processes—helping teams build practical skills around their own products.

Where included in scope, moderated research with disabled and neurodivergent participants can challenge assumptions and uncover previously unidentified barriers. Follow-up testing and validation help teams understand whether changes improve access in practice.

Practical Support That Evolves With Your Needs

Depending on the work agreed, you may receive consultancy sessions, accessibility testing, structured findings, developer-ready recommendations, Jira tickets, research evidence, workshops, remediation guidance and validation—focused on the decisions and improvements your teams need to make.

Digital Accessibility Services

Bespoke accessibility solutions tailored to your service architecture, release cycles, and business context.

Our capabilities combine expert technical evaluation with authentic lived experience, helping teams design, improve, and validate inclusive digital journeys aligned with WCAG 2.2 Level AA, PSBAR 2018, and the European Accessibility Act (EAA).

Capabilities & Programmes

Lived Experience

Inclusive Research & User Testing

Task-based research and moderated testing with disabled and neurodivergent participants using real assistive technologies. We uncover genuine barriers, workarounds and opportunities across key digital journeys.

Enquire about Inclusive Research
Technical Validation

Assistive Technology Health Checks

Expert testing across screen readers including VoiceOver, NVDA and JAWS, alongside Voice Control, magnification, keyboard and switch access. Designed to identify accessibility blockers early and validate how experiences work in practice.

Enquire about AT Health Checks
Compliance & EAA

Accessibility Audits & Action Roadmaps

WCAG 2.2 Level AA and applicable EAA-aligned reviews that combine technical evaluation with practical recommendations. Findings are prioritised and translated into clear, actionable remediation for product and engineering teams.

Enquire about Audits & Strategy
Vendor Assurance

Strategic Procurement & Supplier Testing

Accessibility evaluation of third-party software, vendors, white-label services and digital tools before procurement decisions are made—helping organisations identify accessibility risks and make informed supplier choices.

Enquire about Procurement Testing
Conversion & Opportunity

Granular Journey Discovery & Moderated Research

We go beyond isolated WCAG checks to understand how disabled and neurodivergent people experience digital journeys in practice. Working with our user panel, we examine interactions at a granular level across assistive technologies to uncover friction, workarounds and points of exclusion.

Enquire about Journey Discovery
Innovation & Integrity

AI-Agent Readiness & Human-Verified Assurance

As digital experiences become shaped by AI, we help organisations prepare journeys for emerging AI-agent interactions while keeping real people at the centre. Human-verified testing identifies barriers that synthetic personas and AI simulations overlook.

Capability Building

Accessibility Strategy, Training & Mentoring

Practical guidance, workshops, live assistive technology demonstrations and 1:1 mentoring to build accessibility capability across product, UX, research, design and engineering teams.

Value-Driven Design

Inclusive Design & Unmet User Needs

We turn lived-experience insight into practical product improvements. By uncovering unmet access needs, we help teams create clearer journeys that reduce friction, improve completion rates, and deliver better experiences for everyone.

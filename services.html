Digital Accessibility Services

Bespoke accessibility solutions tailored to your service, product architecture, audience, and release context.

Our services combine technical evaluation with lived experience, helping teams design, improve, and validate inclusive digital journeys aligned with WCAG 2.2 AA, PSBAR 2018, and the European Accessibility Act (EAA).

Capabilities & Programmes

Inclusive Research & User Testing

Task-based research and moderated testing with disabled and neurodivergent participants using real assistive technologies. We uncover genuine barriers, workarounds and opportunities across key digital journeys.

Assistive Technology Testing

Expert testing across screen readers including VoiceOver, NVDA and JAWS, alongside Voice Control, magnification, keyboard and switch access. Designed to identify accessibility blockers early and validate how experiences work in practice.

Accessibility Audits & Action Roadmaps

WCAG 2.2 Level AA and applicable EAA-aligned reviews that combine technical evaluation with practical recommendations. Findings are prioritised and translated into clear, actionable remediation for product and engineering teams.

Strategic Procurement & Supplier Testing

Accessibility evaluation of third-party software, vendors, white-label services and digital tools before procurement decisions are made—helping organisations identify accessibility risks and make more informed supplier choices.

Accessibility Strategy, Training & Mentoring

Practical guidance, workshops, live assistive technology demonstrations and 1:1 mentoring to build accessibility capability across product, UX, research, design and engineering teams.

Lived Experience

Granular Journey Discovery & Moderated Research

We go beyond isolated WCAG checks to understand how disabled and neurodivergent people experience digital journeys in practice. Working with our user panel, we examine interactions at a granular level across assistive technologies to uncover friction, workarounds and points of exclusion that standard testing can miss.

Conversion & Opportunity

Inclusive Design & User Needs

We turn lived-experience insight into practical product improvements. By uncovering unmet access needs and understanding what users are trying to achieve, we help teams create clearer, more inclusive journeys that reduce friction, improve completion and deliver better experiences for everyone.

Innovation & Integrity

AI-Agent Readiness & Human-Verified Assurance

As digital experiences become increasingly shaped by AI, we help organisations prepare journeys for emerging AI-agent interactions while keeping real people at the centre. Human-verified testing helps identify accessibility barriers that synthetic personas and AI simulations can overlook, supporting genuinely usable and accessible digital experiences.

Strategy & Measurement

Lived-Experience Benchmarking & Maturity

Establishing repeatable baseline metrics across core user journeys to measure accessibility progress over time. By combining task completion data with lived-experience testing, we help organisations track product maturity, prioritize engineering investment, and demonstrate clear, value-driven accessibility improvements to stakeholders.

Digital Accessibility Services

Bespoke accessibility solutions tailored to your service architecture, release cycles, and business context.

Our capabilities combine expert technical evaluation with authentic lived experience, helping teams design, improve, and validate inclusive digital journeys aligned with WCAG 2.2 Level AA, PSBAR 2018, and the European Accessibility Act (EAA).

Services

Lived Experience

Inclusive Research & User Testing

Studies with disabled and neurodivergent participants using real assistive technologies to uncover genuine usability barriers across digital journeys.

We go beyond the task. Carefully designed studies give us the structure to explore the wider experience and reveal barriers, workarounds and opportunities that a narrowly defined task would never capture.

We connect the evidence. Drawing on a broad toolkit of research and accessibility methods, we explore what is happening, why it matters and where the experience can be improved — translating lived experience into clear, actionable findings for product, design and development teams.

Technical Validation

Assistive Technology Health Checks

Expert testing across screen readers including VoiceOver, NVDA and JAWS, alongside Voice Control, magnification, keyboard and switch access. Designed to identify accessibility blockers early and validate how experiences work in practice.

We test beyond the obvious. We draw on hands-on experience across assistive technologies to explore how different users access, navigate and interact with digital experiences — identifying compatibility issues, friction and barriers that automated checks or single-technology testing can miss. Findings are translated into clear, practical recommendations for design and development teams.

Compliance & EAA

Accessibility Audits & Action Roadmaps

WCAG 2.2 Level AA and applicable EAA-aligned reviews that combine technical evaluation with practical recommendations. Findings are prioritised and translated into clear, actionable remediation for product and engineering teams.

From findings to action. We turn accessibility findings into structured, ticket-ready work that teams can understand, prioritise and measure. Using AI-assisted workflows alongside robust, repeatable testing frameworks, we help QA, product and engineering teams build accessibility into existing ways of working — from individual features to complex digital ecosystems across global organisations.

Conversion & Opportunity

Granular Journey Discovery & Moderated Research

Exploring digital journeys in depth through carefully planned, moderated research to uncover friction, workarounds and points of exclusion — revealing opportunities to improve the experience for everyone.

Built for continuous improvement. Whether supporting ongoing audits or the development of better user experiences, we test journeys throughout the product lifecycle — from prototypes, content and wording through to design, functionality and interaction. Research can bring together in-house users and disabled testers to understand how experiences work from different perspectives.

Our standardised reporting methodology efficiently catalogues outcomes, making evidence easier to compare, track and act on over time.

Value-Driven Design

Inclusive Design & User-Centred Product Development

We bring lived experience into product development to help teams create clearer, more intuitive and inclusive digital experiences. Working with disabled users from early concepts through to prototypes and live services, we test ideas, language, content and interactions to understand what works, what creates friction and where there is opportunity to add value.

Design with users, not assumptions. We can work alongside product and design teams at different stages of development — exploring concepts, testing prototypes, challenging language and content, and validating journeys before decisions become difficult or costly to change.

Drawing on a broad range of research and accessibility methods, we help teams understand what disabled users need from an experience and translate that understanding into practical product improvements — creating better experiences for users while strengthening the product itself.

Vendor Assurance

Strategic Procurement & Supplier Testing

Accessibility evaluation of third-party software, vendors, white-label services and digital tools before procurement decisions are made — helping organisations identify accessibility risks and make informed supplier choices.

Testing that reflects how the product will actually be used. We can pivot between different products, user needs and procurement requirements — translating business use cases into robust, repeatable tests for competitive evaluation. This creates a reliable basis for comparing suppliers, while testing with real assistive technologies and disabled users to understand accessibility, usability and the wider experience in practice.

Strategy & Measurement

Accessibility Benchmarking & Measurement

Establishing repeatable baseline measures across core user journeys to track accessibility progress over time. Combining task completion data with lived-experience testing provides a practical way to measure improvement, identify recurring barriers and demonstrate the value of accessibility investment.

Measure progress, not just compliance. We create structured, repeatable frameworks that allow organisations to compare experiences over time, across journeys and between releases. By combining quantitative measures with qualitative evidence from disabled users, teams can see where accessibility is improving, where barriers persist and where further investment will have the greatest impact.

Capability Building

Accessibility Strategy, Training & Mentoring

Build accessibility capability across product, UX, research, design and engineering teams through practical guidance, tailored workshops, live assistive technology demonstrations and disabled-user testing with Q&A.

Build capability that lasts. We work alongside teams to turn accessibility from a specialist consideration into something they can confidently apply within their own roles and workflows. Sessions can combine strategy and practical guidance with live demonstrations of assistive technologies, real disabled-user experiences and hands-on exploration of accessibility barriers.

From establishing an accessibility approach and defining responsibilities to mentoring teams through real projects, we help organisations develop the knowledge, confidence and processes to make better accessibility decisions — and keep improving them over time.

Innovation & Integrity

AI-Agent Readiness & Human-Verified Assurance

As digital experiences become shaped by AI, we help organisations prepare journeys for emerging AI-agent interactions while keeping real people at the centre. Human-verified testing identifies barriers that synthetic personas and AI simulations easily overlook.

Prepare for what comes next. As AI tools become more capable, they also expose the small gaps and inconsistencies that can easily be missed in conventional testing. Improving accessibility now strengthens the experience for people today while creating more robust, predictable digital journeys that are better prepared for the AI-enabled tools and interactions of tomorrow.

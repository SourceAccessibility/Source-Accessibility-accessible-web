Digital Accessibility Services

Bespoke accessibility solutions tailored to your service, product architecture, audience, and release context.

Our services combine technical evaluation with lived experience, helping teams design, improve, and validate inclusive digital journeys aligned with WCAG 2.2 AA, PSBAR 2018, and the European Accessibility Act (EAA).

Capabilities & Programmes

Inclusive Research & User Testing

Task-based studies with disabled and neurodivergent participants using real assistive technologies to uncover genuine usability barriers across key user journeys.

Assistive Technology Health Checks

Rapid expert reviews across screen readers (VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS), Voice Control, magnification, keyboard, and switch navigation to catch blockers early in sprint cycles.

Strategic Procurement Testing

Evaluating third-party software, vendors, white-label flows, and digital tools during procurement to guarantee accessibility compliance before financial commitment.

Accessibility Audits & Action Roadmaps

WCAG 2.2 Level AA and EAA-aligned technical reviews that translate findings into clear, ticket-ready remediation tasks for product and engineering backlogs.

Demographic Advocacy & Ageing Population

Representing and championing the digital access needs of older demographics to ensure digital services adapt seamlessly to changing physical and cognitive needs.

Training, Workshops & Mentoring

Hands-on sessions, live screen reader demos, and 1:1 support to build internal accessibility skills across product management, UX design, research, and software engineering.

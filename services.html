Services for accessible product growth

We help teams identify the real barriers in their digital services and turn them into practical, sustainable improvements.

From early-stage strategy to accessibility testing at scale, our services are designed to reduce risk, improve user outcomes, and support confident product decisions.

Discuss your project

What we offer

Accessibility audits

Structured evaluation against WCAG 2.2 AA and EAA-aligned expectations, with expert findings and clear prioritisation for product teams.

Assistive technology reviews

Testing across keyboard navigation, screen readers, magnification, Voice Control, and other assistive tools to catch usage issues that standard checks miss.

Inclusive user research

Task-based research with disabled and neurodivergent participants to understand where design assumptions cause friction and exclusion.

Procurement and vendor testing

Evaluation of third-party tools and suppliers to support better accessibility decisions before purchase, rollout, or contract sign-off.

Accessibility strategy

Support for governance, evidence gathering, team capability, and roadmap planning so accessibility becomes part of the product lifecycle.

Remediation support

Actionable guidance for design and engineering teams to implement improvements efficiently and avoid repeat issues in future releases.

Our approach

We work in partnership with internal teams to create practical and sustainable change. Our process combines expert review, lived-experience testing, and clear communication so your roadmap is based on evidence rather than assumptions.

Every engagement is tailored to your service, audience, and organisational maturity. We focus on identifying the highest-impact barriers first and building a pathway for continuous improvement.

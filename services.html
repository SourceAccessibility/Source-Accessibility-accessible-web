Technical Validation

Assistive Technology Health Checks

Expert testing with screen readers including VoiceOver, NVDA and JAWS, alongside Voice Control, magnification, keyboard and switch access. Designed to identify accessibility blockers early and validate how experiences work in practice.

Test with a light touch. Read more

Hands-on testing across assistive technologies explores how people access, navigate and interact with digital experiences — identifying compatibility issues, friction and barriers that automated checks or single-technology testing can miss.

Testing is performed in-house, with the option to validate findings with disabled users where real-world user verification is needed. Scope can be shaped around your priorities and timelines, from rapid manual checks to more in-depth assessment. Findings are presented in a clear, focused format, with the technical detail needed for remediation alongside concise insight to support product decisions and wider accessibility understanding.

Strategy & Measurement

Accessibility Benchmarking & Measurement

Establish repeatable baseline measures across core user journeys to track accessibility progress over time, identify recurring barriers and understand where further investment can make the greatest difference.

We create structured, repeatable frameworks that allow organisations to compare accessibility across journeys, releases and over time. Innovation & Integrity

AI-Agent Readiness & Human-Verified Assurance

Human-verified testing exposes accessibility and interaction barriers that can affect both people and emerging AI agents, helping organisations build more robust digital journeys for the way services are increasingly accessed.

Prepare for what comes next. Read more

AI personas risk creating an illusion of success when prompts need to anticipate the barrier before the persona can encounter it. The test risks validating what is already known rather than providing genuine discovery.

Our human-verified testing brings real people into the process to uncover unexpected barriers, workarounds and interaction issues that predefined personas and automated checks may not expose.

These findings can reveal weaknesses in structure, labelling, navigation and interaction that affect not only people using assistive technologies, but also how emerging AI agents interpret and navigate digital experiences. Improving accessibility and creating more consistent, well-structured digital journeys can help both people and AI-enabled tools interact with services more reliably — strengthening experiences today while preparing for emerging agent-based interactions.

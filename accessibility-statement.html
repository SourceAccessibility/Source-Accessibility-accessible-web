Accessibility Statement

Source Accessibility Ltd is committed to improving digital accessibility and uses WCAG 2.2 Level AA as its target. This statement describes our approach, the checks carried out on the locally generated website and the limitations that remain.

Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA provide the technical accessibility criteria used to evaluate this website. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA, but do not currently claim full conformance.

Our accessibility approach also takes account of relevant UK legislation and guidance, including the Equality Act 2010 and the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018, where applicable.

Evaluation and testing

An earlier audit of the locally generated website covered all 18 public pages. Its axe-core scan reported zero automated violations against the WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A/AA rules it checks. This result is not a conformance assessment and does not establish that every accessibility barrier has been found. The audit predates subsequent page updates, and the automated scan has not been rerun across the site since those changes.

That audit included structural checks of document language, headings, landmarks, image alternatives, link names, duplicate IDs and local links. Representative keyboard checks covered navigation and service-discovery journeys, including the skip link, header, contact route, footer and video embed. Responsive checks included 320, 768 and 1280 CSS pixel widths and a text-spacing override. A long-heading reflow issue found during those checks was corrected and rechecked across the generated pages.

These checks were limited to the methods and browser used for the local audit. This statement page has also been checked locally at 320 CSS pixels and with a 200% root-text-size simulation; that simulation is not a site-wide browser zoom test.

Known limitations and checks outstanding

The current version has not been fully verified with VoiceOver, NVDA or JAWS, Voice Control, or mobile screen readers. Actual 400% browser zoom and site-wide 200% text resizing have not been comprehensively tested.

The embedded video player and the accuracy and completeness of its captions have not received a full manual review. An earlier browser accessibility-tree inspection exposed a YouTube thumbnail control with the unclear name “thumbnail-image”; third-party player controls can change, so this needs checking in the current player.

These checks remain outstanding. Automated test results should not be taken as proof of WCAG conformance.

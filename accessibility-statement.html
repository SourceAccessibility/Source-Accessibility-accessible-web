Accessibility Statement

Source Accessibility Ltd is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We continually apply WCAG 2.2 AA standards to create an inclusive experience for every user.

Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA defines technical and design requirements for digital platforms.

This website is fully conformant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Full conformance means all content operates cleanly without known technical or interaction barriers across assistive technologies.

Technical Architecture & Compatibility

Our site relies on standard HTML5, CSS3, and ARIA attributes to ensure full compatibility with modern web browsers and assistive tools:

  • Semantic HTML: Native landmarks (<header>, <nav>, <main>, and <footer>) establish logical rotor navigation.
  • Flexible Layouts: CSS Grid and Flexbox support reflow up to 400% zoom without horizontal scrolling.
  • High-Contrast Focus: Focus indicators maintain a minimum 3:1 contrast ratio against background surfaces.

Evaluation & Verification Methodology

Accessibility on this site is verified through manual testing and assistive technology sweeps:

  • Keyboard Navigation: Verified full keyboard operability, focus order, and skip links.
  • Screen Readers: Tested with VoiceOver and NVDA across structural elements, interactive controls, and document links.
  • Voice Control: Verified that visible link text matches programmatic accessible names for seamless voice targeting.

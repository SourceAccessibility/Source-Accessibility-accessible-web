Accessibility shaped by lived experience

Source Accessibility is an independent digital accessibility consultancy based in London, working with product teams across the UK and internationally.

We exist to make digital accessibility practical, human, and achievable. Our work sits between technical standards and real disabled users, combining technical WCAG evaluation with authentic user testing across screen readers, Voice Control, magnification, keyboard-only, and switch access.

Four threads that run through our work

However you work with Source Accessibility, these four core principles are always woven into our partnership:

1. Research & Lived Experience

Task-based research with disabled and neurodivergent participants using real assistive technologies, rather than relying solely on automated checklists.

2. Practical Strategy

Understanding your product, users, and priorities to turn complex findings into clear, ticket-ready recommendations that fit sprint rhythms.

3. From Findings to Action

Delivering evidence-based reporting, video clips, and direct developer guidance so teams know exactly what to fix, why it matters, and how to remediate.

4. Ongoing Partnership

Flexible, continuous support that stays alongside your organisation as your products, design systems, and engineering teams evolve.

Trusted expertise & industry impact

We work with high-traffic global travel platforms, charities, educational institutions, and rebranded digital services:

  • Skyscanner: Managing Skyscanner's Disabled Tester Panel (DTP) and leading accessibility testing programmes across web, app, and partner booking flows.
  • Trefoil Trust: Delivering independent accessibility reviews to remove barriers for grant applicants and parents of disabled children.
  • University of St Andrews: Embedding accessible development principles and lived-experience testing directly into the Computer Science degree syllabus.

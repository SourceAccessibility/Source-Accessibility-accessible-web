Granular Journey Discovery & Moderated Research

Understand the journey, not just the task. In-depth research into digital journeys, interactions and user behaviour to uncover friction, workarounds and points of exclusion—revealing where experiences can be improved.

We investigate how digital experiences work across complete user journeys, examining the interactions, decisions and barriers encountered along the way.

Granular Journey Discovery combines expert-led manual testing, structured measurement and moderated observation, with the option to involve disabled and neurodivergent participants depending on project scope. Lived-experience research can reveal previously unidentified barriers, challenge assumptions and help validate whether improvements genuinely support usability and independence.

What Is Granular Digital Journey Discovery?

We investigate connected steps, decisions and interactions across a digital journey—not just an isolated page or task outcome. Scenario-based testing, expert evaluation and moderated observation help explain where people find information, encounter barriers, recover from errors and how the experience affects confidence and independence. Methods are selected for the research question and product maturity, from prototypes to complex live services.

What We Investigate

Complete Digital Journeys

We examine linked tasks such as discovery, search, account access, forms, booking, payment, support and third-party journeys, including error recovery and confirmation.

Content, Language and Comprehension

We assess whether people can find and understand labels, instructions, error messages and signposting well enough to make informed decisions.

Interaction and Navigation

We examine keyboard, screen reader, Voice Control, magnification, reflow and mobile interactions, including focus, dynamic states and recovery from mistakes.

Design and Product Behaviour

We explore information architecture, prototypes, components, feedback and consistency to understand how design decisions affect access and independence.

From Individual Interactions to Measurable Outcomes

We break complex journeys into realistic scenarios and granular assertions so important interactions can be assessed consistently across agreed devices, assistive technologies and product versions.

Journey Scenarios

Tasks reflect meaningful user goals and decisions across the journey.

Granular Assertions

Checks cover discoverability, understanding, operability, feedback, recovery and task completion.

Consistent Outcomes

Agreed pass, fail and not-tested outcomes are supported by observations explaining the result.

User Impact and Comparable Evidence

Evidence records task completion, independence and effort, making recurring barriers and change over time easier to understand.

The framework is adapted to the project, providing comparable evidence without treating every barrier as equal.

Research Designed Around Your Journey

Expert-led investigation, participant research and follow-up validation can be commissioned individually or combined to suit the research question and project scope.

Expert-Led Journey Discovery

Manual investigation identifies interaction patterns, accessibility barriers and questions for further research.

Moderated Usability Research

Where included in scope, participants use their own assistive technologies and interaction strategies to reveal unexpected barriers, challenge assumptions and assess usability and independence.

Scenario-Based Testing

Realistic tasks examine outcomes and the interactions that contribute to them.

Assistive Technology Journey Testing

Selected screen readers, Voice Control, magnification and keyboard navigation help evaluate combined interactions.

Accessibility Interaction Diagnostics

Focused component testing investigates likely causes and their effect on the wider journey.

Comparative and Repeat Testing

Repeat assessments can track changes across devices, technologies, releases or development cycles.

Selected methods, technologies and validation are agreed for each journey and project scope.

Beyond the Individual Accessibility Issue

Small points of friction can accumulate and prevent independent task completion. We connect individual findings to wider patterns, examining where barriers begin, how workarounds add effort and whether people can recover independently. A technically achievable task is not necessarily accessible or equitable.

Clear Evidence. Practical Action.

We turn observations into evidence teams can understand, compare and act on. Depending on scope, deliverables may include:

Journey and Evaluation Records

  • Journey maps, scenarios and granular assertions.
  • Consistent outcomes and comparative results.
  • Accessibility and usability findings.

Evidence and Recommendations

  • Participant observations, quotes and assistive technology evidence.
  • Reproduction steps, screenshots or recordings where appropriate.
  • User impact, prioritised recommendations and developer-ready or Jira-compatible findings.
  • Remediation, validation guidance or stakeholder readouts as agreed.

Deliverables are agreed for each project; not every engagement includes every item.

When Deeper Journey Research Makes a Difference

Use granular journey discovery to investigate complex transactions, third-party integrations, task abandonment, prototypes, digital transformation and recurring issues across release cycles. Scope can cover a single service or connected digital ecosystem. We support UX and research teams, product managers, accessibility leads, designers, engineers and public-sector organisations making evidence-led decisions.
